Train like you mean it

Real coaching for adults — not just another open gym.

Most adults learn volleyball by playing pickup and picking up habits along the way. Our adult training fixes that. A Boomtown coach runs every session: a focused skill block, then high-rep drills, then live play where you put it to work. You get more quality touches in 90 minutes than in weeks of pickup.

We train beginners to pros. Every session is 90 minutes of coached training, then play time afterward — and that play time is part of the session, not an extra. Your $35 covers both, drop-in fee included.

Who it’s for

If you play, or want to, there’s a spot for you.

Beginners who want to learn the basics without feeling lost in pickup. Returning players who haven’t touched a ball since high school or college. League players getting ready for the women’s, men’s, or co-ed season. Competitive players who want sharper skills before tournaments.

Not sure where you fit? Check our skill levels or just come — coaches split groups by level. Strong high-school players can join adult sessions too; email us first. From first-timers to former college players, we train beginners to pros.

Private & small-group lessons

Want one-on-one time or a session for your team? Our coaches offer private and small-group lessons by request at Boomtown FieldhouseUSA. Email zhu@boomtownvball.com with your level, group size, and the days that work for you.

What you’ll work on

The skills that win rallies in leagues and tournaments.

Ball control

Passing & serve receive

Platform angles, footwork, and reading the server so your first touch goes where your setter needs it.

Hands

Setting

Clean hand sets, location, and tempo. Useful for every position, not just setters.

Attack

Approach & arm swing

A repeatable approach, timing with the set, and shot selection beyond hitting it hard.

Game IQ

Serving & systems

Consistent serves, rotations, covering, and where to stand on defense — the stuff that wins close sets.

How a session runs

90 minutes of coached training, then play time.

  1. Warm-upMovement prep and partner ball control.
  2. Skill focusThe coach teaches one skill and why it works.
  3. High-rep drillsSmall groups, short lines, lots of touches.
  4. Live playGame-like drills and scrimmage to use it under pressure.
  5. Play timePart of the session: keep playing in drop-in — already paid for.

FAQ

Good to know before you train.

Is there adult volleyball training near Denver?

Yes. Boomtown Athletics runs coached adult volleyball training at Boomtown FieldhouseUSA, 14200 E Alameda Ave in Aurora, in the Denver metro. Sessions are Fridays 7–8:30 PM and Sundays 5:30–7 PM.

Are beginners welcome at adult training?

Yes. Beginners are welcome, and coaches split groups by level so newer players and experienced players both get the right reps.

How much does adult training cost?

$35 per person per session. That includes the drop-in fee, so you can keep playing afterward.

How do I sign up for adult training?

Fill out the adult training sign-up form on this page or email zhu@boomtownvball.com.

Do you offer private volleyball lessons for adults?

Yes, by request. Email zhu@boomtownvball.com with your level, group size, and the days that work for you.

What ages can join youth training?

Ages 6 and up. Little Spikers is ages 6–9, Developmental is ages 8–16, and Advanced Skills is 14 and up. Youth training runs through our partner club, Colorado Boom, on Tuesdays and Thursdays starting at 5:00 PM.

How much is youth training?

$295 per month for twice a week or $150 per month for once a week. Colorado Boom club members train both days for $150 per month. The first week is free. Sign up on the Colorado Boom programs page.

Is there play time after adult training?

Yes. Every session is 90 minutes of coached training followed by play time, and the play time is part of the session. Your $35 includes the drop-in fee.

Do you train advanced and former college players?

Yes. We train beginners to pros. Coaches split groups by level, so experienced players get competitive reps while newer players learn the basics.

Do I need a team to join adult training?

No. Come on your own or with friends. Coaches put groups together at each session.

Can high school players join adult training?

Strong high-school players can join adult sessions. Email zhu@boomtownvball.com first.

Can club players from other clubs join youth training?

Yes. Club players are welcome from any club — you don’t need to play for Colorado Boom. Colorado Boom club members can train both days for $150 per month.

Do you accept My Spark Denver?

Yes. My Spark is accepted, and $1,000 paid in full covers 6 months of youth training.

Who coaches youth training?

Colorado Boom coaches run every youth session at Boomtown FieldhouseUSA, in small groups by age and level.

What should I bring to volleyball training?

Court shoes, knee pads, and water. Volleyballs are provided.
